Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a website?

No! Valmont gives you your own store page on ValmontGadgets.com. You don't need to build or host anything.

How do I get paid?

Customers pay you directly via MoMo, cash on delivery, or through Valmont Pay. You keep 100% of your sales — Valmont only charges the monthly subscription.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel your subscription anytime and your store stays active until the end of your billing period.

What if I'm outside Accra?

No problem. Valmont serves all of Ghana. Customers from any region can find your store and contact you. You handle delivery your way.

How does Valmont verify my shop?

We verify your Ghana Card, phone number, and business details. Pro and Enterprise partners also get a physical or video verification visit.

What's the difference between this and just listing on the swap page?

The swap page is for individuals selling their personal phone. The Partner Program is for businesses with inventory. Partners get a dedicated store page, analytics, promoted listings, and the Verified Partner badge.
