🛡️ Stay Safe — Tips for Swapping

✓Meet in a public place — mall, restaurant, or busy area. Never meet alone.
✓Check the IMEI — dial *#06# and verify it matches. Avoid stolen phones.
✓Test everything — screen, cameras, Face ID, speakers, charging port.
✓Check battery health — Settings → Battery → Battery Health. Below 80% = negotiate lower.
✓Bring a friend — don't go alone to meet a stranger.
✓Pay with MoMo — cash is risky. Mobile money leaves a trace.
